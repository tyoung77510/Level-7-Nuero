# LinkedIn Content Strategy
> **Source:** linkedin-content.md, linkedin-assessment-posts.md | **Last Updated:** 2026-07-11

## Strategy Overview
| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Target audience** | Ops Directors, Plant Managers, Engineering Managers, VPs of Ops, COOs, Maintenance Managers at $20M–$500M industrial firms |
| **Tone** | Blunt. Confident. Evidence-based. No corporate speak. Tell hard truths. |
| **Cadence** | 3–4 posts/week: 2 thought leadership + 1 evidence/metrics + 1 engagement/industry |
| **Hashtags** | Mix broad (#ProjectManagement, #Manufacturing) + niche (#ProjectControls, #CMMS, #OperationalExcellence, #EVM) |

## Content Pillars
| Pillar | Purpose | Example Post |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| **Education** | "Here's how this works" | EVM explained in plain language |
| **Evidence** | "Here are the numbers" | Before/after metrics from engagements |
| **Engagement** | "What do you think?" | Polls, hot takes, questions |
| **Industry** | "This is what's happening" | Trends, news, observations |

## Engagement Hooks
- **Questions:** End every post with a question
- **Tag-a-colleague:** "Tag an ops leader who needs to see this"
- **Polls:** "What's your biggest ops headache?"
- **Hot takes:** "Unpopular opinion: 100% PM compliance is a bad target"

## Content Guidelines
**DO:** Call out problem in first sentence. Use specific numbers. Tell stories (anonymized). Reply to every comment.
**DON'T:** Use corporate speak. Sell in the first interaction. Post motivational quotes. Argue in comments.

## Sample Post Drafts
**PMO Post:** *"One project? You don't need a PMO. Five projects with no standard methodology, no governance, and leadership getting different answers from every PM? You've got a problem."*

**CMMS Post:** *"Your CMMS isn't the problem. Your data is. Clean the data first. Then deploy the tool. In that order."*
