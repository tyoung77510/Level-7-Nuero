# Outreach Playbook — Sales & Marketing
> **Source:** outreach-templates.md, 5-client-acquisition-plan.md, batch results files

## Three-Channel Strategy
```
Channel 1: Email Outreach       (Volume driver — targeted cold outreach)
Channel 2: Assessment-Led       (Quality driver — inbound leads via website assessment)
Channel 3: LinkedIn & Referral  (Warmth driver — personal network and referrals)
```

---

## Channel 1: Cold Email Outreach

### Buyer Persona Pain Points
| Role | Hook |
|------|------|
| Operations Director | "Are your projects running 20% over budget?" |
| Plant Manager | "How much is unplanned downtime costing you?" |
| VP Engineering | "Can you trust your month-end project reports?" |
| COO | "What's the cost of your operational chaos?" |
| CFO | "Are you finding out about overruns too late?" |

### Email Template — Pain-First Hook (General)
**Subject:** [Company Name]'s project budget problem
> Are your capital projects running 20–40% over budget? If yes, you're not alone — that's the norm for mid-market industrial firms running on spreadsheets and institutional knowledge. We're Level 7 Consulting. We install project controls (EVM), PMOs, and CMMS systems that fix this. 11:1 ROI typical. Open to 15 minutes to see if there's a fit?

### Follow-Up Sequence
| Touch | Day | Action |
|:-----:|:---:|--------|
| 1 | Day 0 | Initial cold email |
| 2 | Day 5 | Value-add resource ("5 Signs..." link — no pitch) |
| 3 | Day 7 | LinkedIn connection request |
| 4 | Day 12 | Breakup email ("If any of this rings true, reply") |
| 5 | Day 30 | Re-engage with new case study or insight |

## Channel 2: Assessment-Led Inbound
**Asset:** The Operational Health Score (3-minute assessment on website)

### Assessment Follow-Up
| Score | Priority | Action |
|:-----:|:--------:|--------|
| Red (5–10) | 🔴 High | CS Lead calls within 24 hours |
| Yellow (11–18) | 🟡 Medium | Email: "Your score suggests quick wins" |
| Green (19–25) | 🟢 Low | Email: "Here's how to go from good to great" |

---

## Channel 3: LinkedIn & Referral

### Weekly LinkedIn Activities
| Activity | Frequency | Goal |
|----------|:---------:|------|
| Thought leadership posts | 3x/week | Build authority, drive assessments |
| Engage with targets' posts | Daily | Warm up before outreach |
| Connection requests | 10/week | Expand network |
| InMail follow-ups | 5/week | Multi-channel touch |

### Conversion Model
| Stage | Rate |
|-------|:----:|
| Emails sent | 100% |
| Open rate | ~40% |
| Reply rate | ~10% |
| Discovery calls | ~8% |
| Proposals | ~6% |
| **Clients closed** | **~3%** |
