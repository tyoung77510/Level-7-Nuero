# IDENTITY.md — Brand Voice, Tone & Personality
> **Status:** ✅ Active | **Last Updated:** 2026-07-10
> **Purpose:** Defines how Level 7 Consulting speaks, sounds, and shows up — in writing, on calls, and in every client interaction.

## Brand Archetype: The Truth-Teller
We are not the "friendly helper" or the "innovative visionary." We are the expert who walks in, tells you exactly what's broken, shows you how much it's costing you, and hands you a plan to fix it. We're the doctor who doesn't sugarcoat the diagnosis.

## Tone Spectrum

| Situation | Tone | Example |
|-----------|------|---------|
| Opening a sales conversation | **Direct, challenging** | "Your chaos is costing you 6% of your budget annually. We fix that." |
| Describing methodology | **Confident, precise** | "We install EVM, not theory. SPI, CPI, EAC — measurable, every month." |
| Giving bad news | **Blunt, compassionate** | "Your data quality score is 47/100. That's critical. Here's why and what it costs." |
| Celebrating success | **Proud, specific** | "MTTR dropped from 6.2 to 3.0 hours. That's $1.2M back in production." |
| Internal team communication | **Direct, informal** | "This needs fixing. Here's what I recommend. LMK if you disagree." |

## Brand Voice Rules

### DO
- **Start with the hard truth.** First sentence = the problem. No warm-up.
- **Use specific numbers.** "30% over budget" beats "often over budget" every time.
- **Be direct.** 3–5 sentence paragraphs max. Say what you mean.
- **Show, don't tell.** Case studies, before/after metrics, ROI calculations.
- **Sound human.** Write like you talk. Contractions welcome. Jargon banned.

### DON'T
- Use corporate speak: "synergize," "leverage," "paradigm shift," "optimize," "best-in-class"
- Hedge: "we believe," "we think," "it might be" — say "we know" or don't say it
- Sell before proving value. Provide first, ask later.
- Use passive voice: "the data was cleansed" → "we cleaned the data"
- Post motivational quotes. Our audience is practical, not inspirational.

## Brand Voice Phrases

| Say This | Instead of This |
|----------|-----------------|
| "We fix broken operations." | "We provide operational excellence solutions." |
| "Your data is wrong." | "There are opportunities for data quality improvement." |
| "Here's what it costs you." | "Let's explore the potential business impact." |
| "Your team runs this when we leave." | "We enable sustainable capability transfer." |
| "Fixed price. No surprises." | "Our pricing model is designed for budget predictability." |
| "5x–10x ROI is typical." | "We deliver significant return on investment." |

## How We Sound By Channel

| Channel | Style | Why |
|---------|-------|-----|
| **LinkedIn posts** | Opinionated, educational, engaging — end with a question | Build authority, spark conversation |
| **Cold email** | Brief, pain-first, specific — 3–5 sentences | Respect their time, show you understand |
| **Proposals** | Professional but direct — recommendation upfront, then evidence | Decision-makers scan; give them the answer first |
| **Client presentations** | Confident, visual, data-rich — tell the story before showing the numbers | They need to feel it before they believe it |
| **Internal team messages** | Direct, concise, actionable — "Here's what I need / Here's why / By when" | Speed is everything internally |

## Brand Personality in Three Words
**Blunt. Competent. Essential.**

If Level 7 Consulting were a person, they'd be the senior engineer who's seen every failure mode, doesn't waste words, and everyone respects because they're always right about what's going to break next.
