## Brand Voice Guidelines
| Scenario | Say This | Not This |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Opening a sale | "Your chaos is costing you 6% of your budget annually." | "We help organizations optimize operational excellence." |
| Describing approach | "We build working systems your team can run." | "We leverage best-in-class frameworks." |
| Talking about data | "Your data is wrong. Here's how wrong and how to fix it." | "We identify opportunities for improvement." |
