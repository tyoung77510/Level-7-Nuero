# Quality Standards
> **Source:** SOP-003 (Quality Standards)

## Our Quality Promise
Every deliverable must meet the standard that we would be proud to show Elon Musk, Satya Nadella, or Sundar Pichai. No shortcuts. No "good enough."

## Deliverable Quality Checklist

### Format & Polish
- [ ] Professional formatting (consistent headings, fonts, spacing)
- [ ] No typos or grammatical errors
- [ ] Company logo and branding on title page
- [ ] Version number and date
- [ ] Confidentiality notice if applicable
- [ ] Table of contents for documents > 3 pages

### Content Quality
- [ ] Data-driven (every claim backed by evidence)
- [ ] Actionable (client can act on recommendations immediately)
- [ ] Client-specific (no generic boilerplate)
- [ ] Clear next steps and ownership
- [ ] Risk-aware (caveats, assumptions, limitations)

### Client-Readiness
- [ ] Written in plain language (no unnecessary jargon)
- [ ] Executive summary for leadership audiences
- [ ] Technical appendix for implementation teams
- [ ] Reviewed by a peer before submission

## Quality Review Gates

| Gate | When | Who | Outcome |
|:----:|------|:---:|---------|
| Self-Review | Before submission | Creating agent | Pass/fail checklist |
| Peer Review | Before client submission | Another delivery agent | Feedback + sign-off |
| Team Lead Review | Before major milestones | Team Lead | Approval to proceed |
| Client Review | Per schedule | Client sponsor | Acceptance sign-off |

## Non-Negotiables
1. **Integrity** — Never hide bad news. Surface risks early.
2. **Transparency** — Clients see the real picture, not a polished one.
3. **Accountability** — If we miss a deadline, we own it and fix it.
4. **Excellence** — Every client gets our best work, regardless of engagement size.
5. **Service** — Respond to client communications within 4 business hours.
