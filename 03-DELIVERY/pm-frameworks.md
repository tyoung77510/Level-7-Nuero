## Level 7 PMO Maturity Model (Level 1–5)

| Level | Name | Definition | Our Action |
|-------|------|-----------|------------|
| **1** | Initial (Ad-hoc) | No formal PM. Heroes run projects. No templates, no governance. | Stabilize. Basic templates. Single tracker. |
| **2** | Structured (Repeatable) | Basic PM exists. Templates used inconsistently. PMs lack authority. | Build PMO Charter. Formalize governance. Establish decision rights. |
| **3** | Managed (Governed) | Standard method enforced. Stage gates work. PMs trained. Basic EVM. | Optimize. Portfolio reporting. Resource capacity. Full EVM. |
| **4** | Quantitatively Managed (Predictive) | Data-driven decisions. Reliable forecasting. Dashboards drive action. | Institutionalize. PM COE. Continuous improvement loops. |
| **5** | Optimizing | PMO drives org change. Data feeds strategy. PMO is a profit center. | Innovate. Benchmark. AI/automation. |

> **Reality check:** Jump from Level 1 to Level 4 in one engagement is fantasy. Target +1 level per cycle.

## PMO Charter — The Most Important Document
Without it, the PMO will fail. Establishes authority, scope, and operating model.

### Charter Structure
1. **Executive Summary** — Business problem the PMO solves
2. **Vision & Mission** — 3-year vision, daily mission
3. **PMO Services** — What the PMO does (and doesn't do)
4. **Governance Structure** — Decision rights, steering committee, escalation
5. **Resource Model** — Staffing, budget, tooling
6. **Performance Metrics** — How PMO success is measured
7. **RACI Matrix** — Who decides what

## Methodology Selection

| Client Profile | Recommended Methodology |
|----------------|------------------------|
| Capital projects >$5M, regulatory | Waterfall with stage gates |
| Internal improvements, IT, R&D | Agile or Hybrid |
| Mixed portfolio | Hybrid — Agile for discovery, Waterfall for execution |
| Highly uncertain scope | Agile with sprint-based EVM |

## Training & Capability Transfer (SOP-PM-003)
- **Goal:** Client team runs without us after engagement
- **Approach:** Train-the-trainer with hands-on coaching
- **Materials:** Role-based training plans, quick reference guides, video library, sandbox environment
- **Super user network:** 2–3 per client site who serve as in-house experts post-engagement
