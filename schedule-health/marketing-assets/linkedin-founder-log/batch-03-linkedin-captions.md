## Day 035 — Why Ordo7 doesn't fabricate a metric it can't actually compute

A leading schedule-analytics platform just published a 2026 industry report — survey data plus a warehouse of real historic project schedules. Two numbers stood out.

44% of schedule updates in the study carried risky levels of changed actual dates. 32% walked back progress already claimed. Once an activity starts or finishes, that date is supposed to be a fact — not something that gets quietly retuned to match expectations. The same report found teams rate their own schedule quality about 3x better than the data supports: 38% believe they're "high quality," the actual measurement found 13%.

This is exactly why Ordo7 doesn't guess. True cost-based EVM isn't shown because schedule-only exports don't carry cost data — it's marked unavailable, never faked. The schedule-only performance signal Ordo7 does show is scored straight off what's actually in your file, no retrofitting, no rounding up.

An industry report just spent 80 pages proving that self-reported schedule health can't be trusted. That's not a knock on anyone — it's the whole reason an objective read matters.
