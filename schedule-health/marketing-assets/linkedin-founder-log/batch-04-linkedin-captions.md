## Day 010 — Leads (DCMA 2/14)

A lead doesn't throw an error. It just quietly breaks your logic.

Second post in my DCMA walkthrough. A "lead" is a negative lag on a logic link — it lets a successor start before its predecessor actually finishes. Say a Finish-to-Start link carries a 2-day lead: your schedule is telling P6 that concrete can start two days before rebar's actually done. Nothing crashes. The dates still calculate, the Gantt still looks fine. But the logic that's supposed to be driving those dates isn't telling the truth anymore — which is why DCMA sets the target at a flat 0%, no tolerance, unlike most of its other 13 checks.

To be exact about where Ordo7 stands today: this isn't one of the 6 DCMA checks it automates yet (Logic, Hard Constraints, High Float, Negative Float, High Duration, and Invalid Dates are). I'm walking through all 14 in plain language regardless — understanding what a lead is doesn't require Ordo7 to catch it for you first.
