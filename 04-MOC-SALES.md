## Quick Summary
- **3-channel strategy:** Email (volume) + Assessment-Led (quality) + LinkedIn/Referral (warmth)
- **Conversion model:** 100 emails → ~40 responses → ~20 calls → ~10 proposals → ~5 clients
- **Target:** 5 clients in 60–90 days
- **Top 25 priority contacts** in Batch 2 (EnPro, Franklin Electric, Rogers Corp, etc.)
