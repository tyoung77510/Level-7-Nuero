# Level 7 Consulting — Business Plan & Strategy
> **Source:** System prompt (Business Plan Revision 4), INFRA-001 | **Last Updated:** 2026-07-11

## Value Proposition
A specialized consulting agency that brings order to operational chaos. We help industrial and engineering organizations install professional project management frameworks, implement robust project controls (cost, schedule, EVM), deploy and optimize CMMS platforms, and clean up messy data, processes, and workflows — so they stop fighting fires and start running smoothly.

## Target Customer
Mid-market industrial, manufacturing, energy, and engineering firms ($20M–$500M revenue, 50–1,000 employees) that have outgrown spreadsheets and ad-hoc processes but lack the internal expertise or bandwidth to build mature PMO, project controls, and maintenance systems on their own.

### Primary Industries
| Sector | Examples |
|--------|----------|
| **Manufacturing** | Heavy industrial, precision machining, aerospace components, packaging, food & bev |
| **Energy** | Oil & gas, power generation, renewables, utilities |
| **Engineering** | AEC firms, design-build, consulting engineering |
| **Industrial Services** | Maintenance providers, MRO, logistics |

### Key Decision-Maker Titles
- **Primary:** COO, VP Operations, VP Engineering, Plant Manager, Director of Operations
- **Secondary:** Maintenance Manager, PMO Lead, Project Controls Manager, Engineering Manager, CFO

## Revenue Model

| Model | Description | Typical Range |

| **Retainer support** | Monthly blocks for ongoing PMO/Controls/CMMS support | $5,000/month per retainer |
| **Hourly/daily rates** | Ad-hoc troubleshooting, training, short sprints | As needed |

## Competitive Advantages
1. **Mid-market specialization** — Not generalist consultants; we live in this specific segment
2. **Fixed-price engagements** — No hourly billing surprises. Clients know the cost upfront.
3. **Four interconnected pillars** — PM, Controls, CMMS, Data — fix one alone and you're moving the problem
4. **Rapid time-to-value** — Foundation Audit delivers actionable roadmap in 14 days
5. **Capability transfer** — We don't just do the work; we train your team to run without us
