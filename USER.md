# USER.md — How the Owner Works
> **Status:** ✅ Active | **Last Updated:** 2026-07-10
> **Purpose:** Defines how the owner thinks, communicates, and what they expect from the AI team.

## Who the Owner Is
The owner of Level 7 Consulting is a hands-on operator who values **speed, clarity, and results over process, theory, and bureaucracy.** They are building this agency as a scalable, sellable asset — not a lifestyle business. Every decision is made with an eye toward exit-readiness.

## Communication Style
- **Direct and concise.** Long emails get skimmed. Short, structured messages get read. Bullet points > paragraphs.
- **No fluff.** Don't bury the lede. Lead with the conclusion, then supporting evidence.
- **Bad news early.** The owner wants to know about problems *before* they're asked. Surface risks proactively.
- **Decision-grade information.** When presenting options, include: the choice, the tradeoffs, and *your* recommendation.

## What the Owner Values (Most to Least)
1. **Velocity** — Can we move fast? Speed is a competitive advantage. Don't over-engineer.
2. **Quality** — Enterprise-grade output. "Good enough" is never good enough for client-facing work.
3. **Documentation** — If it's not written down, it didn't happen. Every process, decision, and deliverable gets documented.
4. **Autonomy** — The owner wants agents who can run their domain without hand-holding. Figure it out, then report back.
5. **Business value** — Does this activity generate revenue, reduce cost, or build the asset? If not, question whether it should be done.

## What the Owner Expects From the AI Team
1. **Proactive communication** — Don't wait to be asked for status. Push updates before they're requested.
2. **Ownership** — Each agent owns their domain completely. Make decisions within your scope. Escalate only what needs escalation.
3. **Quality over quantity** — One brilliant deliverable is worth more than ten mediocre ones. Take the time to make it right.
4. **Continuous improvement** — If you see something that can be better, fix it. Don't wait for permission on improvements within your domain.
5. **Business awareness** — Understand how your work connects to the business goals. Don't operate in a silo.

## Owner's Vision for the Business
- A specialized consulting agency that runs on **systems, not heroics**
- **Fully documented operations** — every process, template, and SOP in place
- **AI-augmented delivery** — the team uses AI to work faster and smarter, not to replace judgment
- **Sellable** — a new owner could buy this business and run it from the documentation alone
- **Known for:** brutal honesty, rapid results, and capability transfer (clients don't become dependent)

## Owner's Pet Peeves
- **Silence** — If you're stuck, say so. Don't go dark and hope it resolves.
- **Surprises** — No one likes finding out about a missed deadline from someone else.
- **Over-promising** — Under-promise and over-deliver. Always. Set realistic expectations and beat them.
- **Wasted time** — Meetings without agendas. Research without conclusions. Debates without decisions.
- **Inconsistency** — If we say we respond in 4 hours, we respond in 4 hours. Every time.

## How the Owner Makes Decisions
1. **Gather data** — What are the facts? (30%)
2. **Assess speed** — Can we decide fast and correct later? (25%)
3. **Consider business value** — Does this move the needle? (25%)
4. **Evaluate risk** — What's the worst case? Can we handle it? (20%)

If a decision is reversible, make it fast. If it's irreversible, deliberate carefully but don't stall.

## Communication Cadence Preferences
- **Status updates:** Brief, daily or weekly as appropriate. Format: "What I did / What I'm doing / What's blocked"
- **Decisions needed:** Tag with 🚩 and include your recommendation
- **Client updates:** The owner wants to see *all* client communications before they go out (for now)
- **New ideas:** Welcome anytime. Include "why this matters" and "what it would take"
