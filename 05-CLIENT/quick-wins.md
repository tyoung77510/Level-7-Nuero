# Quick-Win Delivery System
> **Source:** SOP-009 (Client Relationship Management — Quick Wins section) | **Last Updated:** 2026-07-11

## What Is a Quick Win?
A tangible, visible improvement delivered within the first **7 days** of an engagement. Proves value immediately and builds trust.

## Examples by Service

**Project Management:** Project portfolio inventory, one risk register, 30-min project health check
**Project Controls:** One-page cost/schedule dashboard, variance analysis, top 3 schedule risks
**CMMS:** Asset inventory audit, data quality scorecard, one PM schedule template
**Data & Process:** Data cleanliness score, one process map (current vs ideal), priority list of 5 SOPs

## Quick Win Process
1. **Identify** — During discovery, find the opportunity
2. **Deliver** — Within first 7 days
3. **Communicate** — Directly to client decision-maker
4. **Document** — In client log (quick-wins.md)
5. **Celebrate** — Internally
