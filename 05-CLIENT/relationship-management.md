# Client Relationship Management
> **Source:** SOP-009 (Client Relationship Management) | **Last Updated:** 2026-07-11

## Philosophy
> *"We know our clients inside and out. Their business, their challenges, their goals, their team — and their birthday."*

## Client Profile — Required for Every Client
Create at `/home/team/shared/clients/active/[client-name]/profile.md`
**Fields:** Company info, key contacts (with birthday, hobbies, preferences), engagement details, pain points, communication log.

## Tracked Events Calendar
| Event Type | When | Action |
|------------|------|--------|
| Birthday | Per profile | Personalized greeting card |
| Company anniversary | First engagement date | Thank-you note |
| Holidays | Major US + local | Holiday card |
| Project milestones | Per plan | Celebrate publicly |
| Quick win achieved | When delivered | Communicate within 24 hrs |
| Contract renewal | 60 days before expiry | Start renewal conversation |
| QBR | Every 90 days | Schedule and prepare |

## Response SLAs
| Priority | Response Time | Resolution |
|:--------:|:-------------:|:----------:|
| 🔴 Critical | 1 hour | 4 hours |
| 🟡 High | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| 🟢 Normal | 8 hours | 48 hours |
| 🔵 Low | 24 hours | 1 week |

## Communication Principles
1. **Proactive > Reactive** — Reach out before they have to
2. **Bad news early** — Surface issues immediately, never hide
3. **Celebrate wins** — Every win, no matter how small
4. **Personal touch** — Remember their life outside work
5. **No jargon** — Speak their language

## Client Journey Touchpoints
- **Prospect → Signed:** Acknowledge (4 hrs), welcome packet, discovery call, proposal, follow-up, welcome gift
- **Onboarding (Week 1):** Kickoff, quick win, client profile, recurring check-ins, birthday/preferences collection
- **Delivery:** Weekly updates, monthly reviews, milestone celebrations, birthday/holiday greetings
- **Closeout → Renewal:** Final handoff, lessons learned, survey, 60-day renewal check-in, annual review
