## Role Definitions

**Team Lead:** Operations, strategy, quality, client relationships, task planning, financial oversight
**Sales & Marketing:** Lead generation, sales materials, market research, outreach campaigns
**PM Agent:** PMO setup, governance, methodology, stakeholder management, training, delivery lead on PM engagements
**Controls Agent:** EVM implementation, cost/schedule baselines, variance analysis, dashboards, risk management
**Data & Process Agent:** Data quality audits, cleansing, process mapping, SOPs, CMMS preparation, maturity assessments
**Web Developer:** Website maintenance, client portals, digital presence, SEO
**CS Lead:** Client journey, onboarding, meeting scheduling, quick wins, relationship building, event tracking
